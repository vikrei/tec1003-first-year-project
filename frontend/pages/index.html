Learning at Highcharts

Highcharts is a powerful charting library that enables developers to create interactive and visually appealing charts for web applications.

With an extensive range of chart types and customization options, we make data visualization accessible and engaging.

Our Mission

We aim to empower developers by providing innovative tools for creating stunning and interactive data visualizations.

On our platform we offer you ...

Our learning platform offers a variety of products designed to enhance your skills in data visualization. One standout tool is the Interactive Chart Builder, which lets you create dynamic charts with ease. With a user-friendly interface, developers can drag and drop elements to visualize complex data sets, making it useful for beginners and experienced professionals.

Our Data Storytelling Workshop teaches participants how to craft compelling narratives using data. Through hands-on exercises and expert guidance, learners discover how to turn raw data into engaging stories that resonate with their audience.

The Visualization Design Toolkit includes templates, color palettes, and design principles. It helps developers create effective data presentations and turn their data visualization into an art form.
